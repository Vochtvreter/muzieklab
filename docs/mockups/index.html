Muzieklab

A rhythm game for reading and hearing rhythms. Pick your meters, note values, rests and number of bars, then tap the rhythm in time or write down what you hear. Every exercise is generated fresh from the rules of meter and notation.

How an exercise is made and judged

A generated 4-bar exercise with its phrase plan

The generator first picks a form, here a a' b a. Bar a' is a variation of a by diminution (the half note split into two quarters). The last bar ends on a long note on a strong beat, so the exercise sounds finished.

Settings→Form plan→Fill along the metric tree→Variations→Musical checks→Speller: ties, beams, rests→Notation

Metric weights in 4/4

Every sixteenth position has a weight. Notes favour strong positions, rests favour weak ones, and syncopation is measured against these weights.

Judging windows

Each tap is matched to the nearest expected note. Windows shrink when notes are close together, so two sixteenths can't be confused.

Variation operators

DiminutionSplit one note into two of half the length.
AugmentationMerge two neighbouring notes inside a beat group.
Rest substitutionSwap a note on a weak position for a rest.
Cell swapReplace one beat's figure with another of similar difficulty.
